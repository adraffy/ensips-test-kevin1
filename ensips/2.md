# ENSIP-2: Hash Registrar

## Abstract

This ENSIP defines a registrar contract for the .eth top-level domain that uses a hash commitment scheme to prevent front-running.

## Motivation

Without a commitment scheme, miners or observers could front-run name registrations by submitting their own transactions with higher gas prices.

## Specification

The registration process consists of two phases:

### Phase 1: Commit

The user submits a hash of their desired name combined with a secret value.

### Phase 2: Reveal

After a waiting period, the user reveals the name and secret, completing registration.

## Rationale

The two-phase commit-reveal scheme provides a simple and effective mechanism for preventing front-running of name registrations.
